# Research Hypotheses & Product Principles

This document articulates the 5 core testable hypotheses developed by the **UX Research Lead (Asan Limbu)** and the team following qualitative research synthesis. Each hypothesis directly addresses a specific onboarding pain point identified during participant interviews.

---

## Hypothesis 1: Structured 30-Day Phasing
> **We believe that** organizing onboarding into structured Day 1, Week 1, and Month 1 roadmap phases  
> **will help new starters** avoid cognitive overload and feel early momentum  
> **because** 100% of interviewed new hires reported feeling overwhelmed by initial document dumps on their first day.

## Hypothesis 2: Transparent "Because..." Microcopy
> **We believe that** providing an explicit "Why this task" rationale for every recommended step  
> **will help new starters** complete onboarding tasks with confidence and trust  
> **because** research shows users ignore recommendations they do not understand, and transparency eliminates hesitation.

## Hypothesis 3: Integrated Buddy & Support Reachability
> **We believe that** surfacing assigned buddy and manager profiles directly alongside tasks  
> **will help new starters** resolve questions 50% faster  
> **because** new hires report reluctance to ask questions when they don't know who owns a specific domain.

## Hypothesis 4: Visual Progress Engagement
> **We believe that** real-time visual progress rings and completion milestone celebrations  
> **will help new starters** maintain high engagement throughout their first month  
> **because** visible progression provides positive feedback and reinforces accomplishment.

## Hypothesis 5: Deterministic Restrained Assistant
> **We believe that** a local, deterministic Q&A assistant with grounded source links  
> **will help new starters** find information in under 3 clicks without distrusting the answers  
> **because** black-box AI chatbots induce skepticism when answers cannot be verified against company policies.
