# Success Metrics & Evaluation Framework

This document defines the key quantitative and qualitative success benchmarks used to evaluate the **HiveMind: Smart Onboarding** prototype during Week 4 usability testing sessions.

Owned by the **UX Research Lead (Asan Limbu)**, with technical feasibility review by the **Technical/Feasibility Lead (Anita Rahman)**.

---

## 1. Quantitative Benchmark Targets

| Metric ID | Performance Metric | Target Benchmark | Benchmark Rationale (ISO 9241-11 Standards) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1: Orientation Speed** | Time-to-First-Task | **≤ 45 seconds** | Measures initial cognitive load and immediate value perception upon landing. |
| **M2: Task Completion Rate** | Unprompted Task Success Rate | **≥ 90%** | Industry standard benchmark for core SaaS self-serve onboarding workflows. |
| **M3: Navigation Efficiency** | Maximum Clicks to Key Info | **≤ 3 clicks** | Verifies information architecture depth for finding buddies, managers, and policies. |
| **M4: System Usability (SUS)** | System Usability Scale Score | **≥ 80.0 / 100** | Corresponds to an 'A' grade (90th percentile) on the Sauro-Lewis SUS benchmark scale. |
| **M5: Clarity & Trust Rating** | Recommendation Trust Index | **≥ 4.5 / 5.0** | Evaluates participant confidence in the "Because..." reasoning microcopy. |

---

## 2. Metric Verification Methodology

### M1: Orientation & First Impressions
- **Task**: Participant reviews the landing page for 30 seconds and states the product's core value proposition and expected next action.
- **Success Threshold**: Accurately describes Smart Onboarding and identifies "Start my first day" in ≤ 45s without guidance.

### M2: Unprompted Task Completion Rate
- **Task**: Participant completes 5 structured tasks (Sign Up/Role Personalisation, Finding Next Task, Marking Task Complete, Locating Buddy/Manager, Finding Holiday Policy).
- **Success Threshold**: Task completed correctly without hints or facilitator intervention.

### M3: Information Search & Retrieval Depth
- **Task**: Participant is asked to locate their assigned Buddy/Manager and find the Holiday & Leave Policy document.
- **Success Threshold**: Target reached in 3 or fewer clicks from the main dashboard navigation.

### M4: System Usability Scale (SUS)
- **Tool**: Standardized 10-item Likert scale (1-5) administered immediately post-session.
- **Calculation**: Odd items `(Score - 1)`, Even items `(5 - Score)`, multiplied by 2.5 to yield a total score out of 100.

### M5: Recommendation & Progress Confidence Index
- **Tool**: 5-point Likert scale question post-task: *"How confident do you feel about your first month based on the clear reasoning provided for your roadmap?"*

---

## 3. Evaluation Environment

- **Sample Size**: 5 participants ($N=5$) with professional workplace experience (diverse backgrounds: engineering, design, marketing, HR admin, finance).
- **Test Platform**: Desktop viewport ($1440 \times 900$), standard Chrome browser.
- **Session Duration**: 20–25 minutes per participant.
