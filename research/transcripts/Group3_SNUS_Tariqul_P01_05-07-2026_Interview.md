AI helper trust

[11:35] Interviewer: Have you used AI tools at work, such as chatbots, AI search, or Copilot-style tools? What was good or bad about it?

[11:45] Participant P01: Not really no. We don't use stuff like Ai there. I use personally use ChatGPT on my phone or laptop to write emails or essays and other stuff, it’s good for saving time in terms of writing, but not really useful for waiting tables.

[12:05] Interviewer: If an AI tool gives you an answer at work, what would make you trust it? 

[12:12] Participant P01: I don’t really trust Ai in general cause I’ve heard horror stories about Ai hallucinating anything it wants to support its own argument, but I’d be more inclined to trust it if it pointed to a specific document in our restaurant that we maybe uploaded to it . Like, if I asked it if the batter for the fried king prawns is and it told me yes, and showed me a little paragraph or link to the kitchen's recipe sheet from where it found this information, because at the end of the day, you have people’s lives at stake here with allergies.

[13:00] Interviewer: What kinds of workplace questions should an AI tool answer with "You should ask a human"?

[13:30] Interviewer: Would you worry about privacy if an AI tool used internal documents or employee data? What would reassure you?
