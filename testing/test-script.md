# Usability test script

Owned by the UX Research Lead (Asan Limbu). Run with 3 to 5 people who have
workplace experience, ideally not students, people who understand workplace
onboarding, finding information at work, and HR or IT support needs. Each
session runs 20 to 30 minutes. Record with consent so a participant note can
be written up afterward using the template in
[`testing/participant-notes/`](participant-notes).

The four tasks below trace back to real pain points already on record from
the team's Week 1 interviews, not invented ones: not knowing who to ask,
information scattered across places, and wanting a checklist specific to your
own role came up directly in
[`research/transcripts/Group3_SNUS_Bushra_P01_2026-07-03_Interview.md`](../research/transcripts/Group3_SNUS_Bushra_P01_2026-07-03_Interview.md).
Score each task against the four measures in
[`docs/success-metrics.md`](../docs/success-metrics.md).

## Intro and consent

"Thanks for making time for this. I am a student working on a smart
onboarding prototype called HiveMind, and I want to see how it feels to use,
not test you. Is it okay if I record this so I can write up notes afterward?
This will take about 20 to 30 minutes. You can skip anything or stop at any
time."

## Context to give the participant

"Imagine you have just joined a company as a new starter. This prototype is
meant to guide your first thirty days: what to do, who to ask, and where to
find things. I will give you a few things to try, and I want to hear your
thoughts as you go."

## Tasks

1. **Get set up.** "You have just joined as a designer, engineer, or
   marketer, whichever feels closest to your own work. Get yourself set up
   in the app." (Landing through the personalise wizard: role, start date,
   learning style, into the dashboard.) Watch for hesitation on the role
   choice and whether the point of each step is clear before they tap it.

2. **Find today's task and mark it done.** "Find something you are meant to
   do today, open it, and mark it complete." (Dashboard into task detail,
   the quick checklist, Mark complete.) Watch for whether the day's priority
   is obvious at a glance, and whether marking progress feels like it
   registered.

3. **Find out who to ask.** "You are not sure who to ask about your work
   schedule. Find out." (People and support screen: buddy, manager, team, or
   the help channel.) This is the task most directly grounded in research:
   the participant in Bushra's Week 1 interview named exactly this as their
   top problem, not knowing whom to ask or where to find things.

4. **Find a key document.** "Find your company's holiday and leave policy."
   (Documents screen, policy category.) Watch how many places they check
   before finding it.

## Closing questions

"What were the top two problems you ran into today?"

"What would you change to make this easier?"

"On a scale of 1 to 5, how confident do you feel about what you would do on
your first day, having just seen this?"

"Thank you so much for your time."

## Notes for whoever runs a session

Write the participant up in
[`testing/participant-notes/`](participant-notes) using the template there,
right after the session while it is fresh. Use a participant ID such as P01,
never the participant's real name, matching the convention already used in
`research/transcripts/`.
